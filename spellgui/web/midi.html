how the key is written

<status>/<data1>: 144/60 = note on channel 1 note 60, 128/60 = the same key released, 176/1 = CC 1 of channel 1. The channel is already in the status (channel 2 = 145 / 177).

In the args, "$" becomes the 0..1 value of the key and "$<n>" becomes round(value × n) — "$127" is the raw MIDI byte and "$255" is the DMX level.

The same key also reaches the graph as midi:<key>, for the in.midi node.
