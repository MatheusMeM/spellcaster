Design system of the projector

System of this tool only. Values in tokens.css; written rule in SISTEMA.md. The program is the 3D model of the device: rear panel is menu, open lid is preferences, the wall is the output.

material aluminium + beam
accent #38FF5C

01Principle

  • Rear panel = menu. Every control lives in a connector, switch or display that exists on the real equipment.
  • Lid open = preferences. Calibration, kpps, scan limits, ILDA in/out.
  • The wall is the output. An ILDA preview is not a UI rectangle: it is a beam in haze.
  • Function before UI. Every element answers "which part of the laser is this". If the answer is "it is a card", cut it.
  • One material. Opaque black anodized aluminium. The only thing that transmits light is the haze.
  • CLI always visible. Whoever learns the GUI learns the spell verb for free.

11Movement, sound and text

Splash, once
  • The focus leaves the static point and goes to the wall, ignoring the laser and the rear panel.
  • The beam outlines SPELLCASTER LASER in a dynamic movement.
  • The letters are revealed and stay on the wall.
  • They all glow when the outline closes.
  • The camera flies to the rear panel (menu) or to the lid (preferences).
  • The laser goes dark and the room light drops.
Sound
  • Square wave jingle in the splash. No sample.
  • Blip when a letter is revealed and when a LEARN is captured.
  • Dry physical-button click on .lb.
  • Descending chord when disarming or entering SCAN FAIL.
  • Mute is a default that is respected and persisted.
Pino's voice
  • Good: Interlock open. No interlock, no beam.
  • Bad: Oops! Looks like something went wrong :(

12Accessibility

  • Visible focus mandatory on every control, including inside the 3D scene: white 2px outline, 2px offset. Tab order follows the reading of the panel.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: no animated splash, no LEARN pulse, no panel transition. All the information remains present through color and text.
  • Contrast — approved pairs over --bg/--panel: --fg, --las, --amb, --oled, and --dim only for a label. --red approved for icon and border; long text in red, no.
  • Minimum click target of 24px in the scene, even when the modelled part is smaller.

13What NOT to do

  • Boxy software. A grid of cards, a sidebar, an app title bar. If it looks like a dashboard, it is wrong.
  • Transparent panel. Glass, blur, translucency in the UI. The only material that transmits light is the haze.
  • Element without a function. Two interlocks, a decorative fuse, an LED that indicates nothing. One interlock. No fuse.
  • Cartoon. Thick stroke, pastel color, cute mascot. Pino is dry Win98.
  • A third font, a fourth accent color, spacing outside the scale, a timing outside the three.
  • Glow as card elevation. Glow is beam.
  • Color alone carrying state.
