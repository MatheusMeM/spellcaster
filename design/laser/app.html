The program is still the projector. It opens looking at the wall: the laser traces the name, the letters light up, and the camera goes to the menu. Only after the key switch arms does the show view unlock.

01what changed from round 4

  • Real rear panel. Layout inspired by the Clubmax, no fuse and with a single interlock. Every port is still what it is: ILDA IN loads the .ild, NET opens NDI · Spout · Art-Net · sACN, DMX IN gives the address, the key switch arms, the OLED with encoder and BACK is the panel menu.
  • Inside, nothing flying. A natural-aluminium optical bench bolted to the bottom, one single block; on it the three modules on mounts, two dichroics and the fold mirror in kinematic holders with adjustment screws, the shutter that closes without the key switch, and the galvo block on an angle bracket with both motors. Drivers and PSU sit on the walls, dark, with cables running from point to point.
  • Beam in GLSL. Instanced cylinders with core and halo, animated dust, additive, bloom. The whole optical path lights up when the key switch arms; the shutter cuts it.
  • SolidWorks camera. The middle button orbits around whatever you clicked; Ctrl pan, Shift zoom, wheel at the cursor (SolidWorks direction, with a toggle in the bindings), arrows, F, Ctrl+1..7.
  • Bindings. B opens the table: any action becomes a key or a MIDI event (note or CC, with channel). The state goes back to the controller (LED and fader feedback). All persisted.
  • Pino 3D. A DMX cable plugged into DMX OUT, with the male end standing on top of the case. The pins are real buttons in the scene.
  • Equivalent command: spell ilda play show.ild --kpps 30; every port and component has its own, always in the corner.
  • Round 6 · orchestrator. The projector is the graph's laser/1 module: every action has an address (laser/1/kpps, laser/1/limit/r, laser/1/net/sacn…) next to the label in BINDINGS, and every binding is a route midi/cc:1:7 → lag → laser/1/kpps. Pino's pin 3 opens the ORCHESTRATOR panel with the manifest (module.json), the active routes blinking when the action runs, and the graph section of the .spell to copy. Command: spell graph add laser/1.

025E arc

  • Excitement · the camera aims at the output; a parked red dot.
  • Entry · the galvo traces SPELLCASTER LASER; the letters light up; the laser goes out and the room light drops; the camera lands on the rear panel.
  • Engagement · arm the key switch, the show unlocks; ports, lid, components, bindings.
  • Exit · turn the key switch: the shutter closes, closing chord.
  • Extension · bindings and kpps remembered on the next launch.
